Học bổng Sunflower
==================

Wed, 27/09/2017 - 10:16

Nhà trường có nhận được email thông tin về học bổng Sunflower,  nguyên văn thông điệp từ quỹ học bổng như sau:

Each year, eSilicon Vietnam works with the Sunflower Mission Foundation to support and manage the Engineering & Technology Scholarship for Excellence Program. This program offers scholarships to Vietnamese undergraduate students in a circuit design engineering or software development related discipline, from second to final year.

The program aims to recognize students demonstrating excellence in the fields of science, technology, engineering and math (STEM) and to encourage outstanding global citizenship.

**Program Summary in 2017**

· Planned to award up to 60 scholarship winners who will receive a scholarship grant of 6.5 Million VND.

· eSilicon will offer special prizes including for top students, top essay and top female engineer.

· The top award winners will also receive an internship/graduate employment offer with eSilicon.
